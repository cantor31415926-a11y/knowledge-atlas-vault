### more than just 的妙用

**个人成长：学习过程**

学习一门语言不仅是记忆词汇，更是通往新文化、新视角和更深刻理解世界的钥匙。

Learning a language is more than just memorizing vocabulary; it's about opening doors to new cultures, perspectives, and a deeper understanding of the world.


**人际关系：沟通本质**

积极倾听不仅是听语言，更是理解情感、认可经历、搭建共情桥梁。

Active listening is more than just hearing words; it's about truly understanding emotions, acknowledging experiences, and building bridges of empathy


**职业认知：工作的意义**

当老师不仅是讲课，更是塑造年轻心灵、激发好奇心、为未来播下希望之种。

Being a teacher is more than just delivering lectures; it's about shaping young minds, arousing curiosity, and planting seeds of hope for the future.
