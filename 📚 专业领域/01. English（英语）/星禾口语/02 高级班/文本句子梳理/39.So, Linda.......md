## 1.what have you been doing with yourself?

**Meaning:**  
a casual way to ask someone **what they have been doing recently**

**中文：**  
你最近都在忙些什么？  
你最近过得怎么样，都在做什么？

**Course sentence:**  
What have you been doing with yourself since graduation?

**Common collocations:**

- what have you been doing lately? — 你最近在忙什么？
- what have you been up to? — 你最近在干嘛？
- keep yourself busy — 让自己忙起来
- do something with yourself — 做点事情 / 安排自己的生活

**My sentence:**  
What have you been doing with yourself recently? I’ve been learning AI and finance.

你最近都在忙些什么？我最近一直在学习 AI 和金融。

**Key point:**  
这里的 **with yourself** 不要直译成“和你自己一起”。

整个表达 **what have you been doing with yourself?** 是比较口语、自然的说法，重点就是：

> **“你最近都在干嘛？”**

## 2.Not much.

**Meaning:**  
not doing anything very special or important

**中文：**  
没干什么；没什么特别的；最近就那样

**Dialogue:**

**A:** What have you been doing with yourself?  
**B:** Not much. Just studying and relaxing.

**中文：**

A：你最近都在忙些什么？  
B：没什么特别的，就学习、休息一下。

**More natural answers:**

- Not much. — 没什么
- Not much, really. — 其实没什么
- Nothing much. — 没干什么特别的
- Not a lot. — 没做太多

这里 **Not much** 是非常常见的口语回答。

## 3.on earth

**Meaning:**  
used to add strong emphasis, especially in questions

**中文：**  
到底；究竟；世上

**Course sentence:**  
Why on earth would a company ignore such an important risk?

**Common collocations:**

- what on earth — 到底是什么
- why on earth — 到底为什么
- how on earth — 到底怎么
- where on earth — 到底在哪里
- who on earth — 到底是谁

**My sentence:**  
How on earth can AI analyze so much financial data so quickly?

AI **到底是怎么**这么快分析这么多金融数据的？

**Key point:**  
这里的 **on earth** 通常不要直译成“在地球上”。

比如：

> What on earth are you doing?

更自然的中文是：

> **你到底在干什么？**

## 4.I think with all that hopping around, I'd be too exhausted to interact with anyone.

**Meaning:**  
I think moving around so much would make me too tired to talk to or spend time with anybody.

**中文：**  
我觉得如果一直这样到处奔波，我会累得根本没精力和任何人交流。

**Key expressions:**

- **with all that hopping around** — 因为一直到处跑、四处奔波
- **be exhausted** — 精疲力尽
- **too ... to ...** — 太……以至于不能……
- **interact with anyone** — 和任何人交流 / 互动

**Course sentence:**  
With all that traveling, I'd be too exhausted to interact with anyone.

**Common collocations:**

- hop around — 到处跑；频繁换地方
- feel exhausted — 感到筋疲力尽
- interact with anyone — 和任何人交流
- interact with customers — 和客户互动
- be too tired to talk — 太累了，没力气说话

**My sentence:**  
After studying and working all day, I'd be too exhausted to interact with anyone at night.

学习和工作一整天之后，晚上我可能会**累得不想和任何人交流**。

**Key point:**  
这里的 **anyone** 比 **people** 语气更强一点。

> interact with people  
> = 和人交流

> interact with anyone  
> = **和任何人都不想交流**

所以这句话更强调：

> **我已经累到谁都不想理了。**

## 5.more than just dancing

**Meaning:**  
something is not only one activity; it has a deeper purpose or includes more things

**中文：**  
不仅仅是跳舞；不只是……而已

**Key expression:**

- **more than just + noun / doing something**  
    = 不仅仅是……

**Course sentence:**  
Learning AI is more than just learning programming skills.

**Common collocations:**

- more than just a job — 不仅仅是一份工作
- more than just a hobby — 不仅仅是一个爱好
- more than just learning — 不仅仅是学习
- more than just money — 不仅仅是钱
- more than just a tool — 不仅仅是一个工具

**My sentence:**  
Building a business is more than just making money; it is about creating value.

创业不仅仅是赚钱，更重要的是创造价值。

**Key point:**  
这里的 **just** 表示“仅仅”。

所以：

> Dancing is more than just exercise.

不是：

> 跳舞超过了运动 ❌

而是：

> 跳舞不仅仅是运动，它还有其他意义。 ✅

类似表达：

- **more than just dancing**  
    = 不只是跳舞（可能还有社交、艺术表达、锻炼等）
    
- **more than dancing**  
    = 比跳舞更多（强调数量或范围）
    

在口语中 **more than just...** 非常常见，用来强调“背后的价值”。

## 5.you also learn how to be more confident, and how to interact better with other people

**Meaning:**  
You learn not only a skill, but also how to become more confident and communicate with others more effectively.

**中文：**  
你还会学会如何变得更加自信，以及如何更好地与别人交流。

**Key expressions:**

- **learn how to do something** — 学会如何做某事
- **be more confident** — 变得更加自信
- **interact better with other people** — 更好地与他人互动 / 交流

**Course sentence:**  
Students learn how to be more confident and how to interact better with other people.

**Common collocations:**

- learn how to communicate — 学习如何沟通
- become more confident — 变得更加自信
- interact with others — 与他人交流
- improve social skills — 提升社交能力
- work better with people — 更好地与他人合作

**My sentence:**  
Studying business helps me learn how to communicate better and interact with different people.

学习商业知识帮助我学会如何更好地沟通，并与不同的人交流。

**Key point:**  
这里两个 **how to** 是并列结构：

> learn **how to be more confident**  
> = 学会如何变得更自信

> learn **how to interact better with other people**  
> = 学会如何更好地与别人交流

另外：

**interact** 比 **talk** 更正式。

- talk with people → 和别人聊天（普通交流）
- interact with people → 和别人产生有效互动（包括沟通、合作、建立关系）

在商业、职场、AI课程里经常出现。
