## 1.I used to help her in the kitchen whenever I had the chance.

**Meaning:**  
I helped her in the kitchen regularly in the past whenever there was an opportunity.

**中文：**  
我过去常常在厨房帮她，只要有机会我都会这样做。

---

## Key expressions:

### 1. **used to do**

= something happened regularly in the past but may not happen now

**过去常常做某事**

例如：

- I used to play basketball when I was young.  
    我小时候经常打篮球。

注意：

**used to + 动词原形**

✅ used to help  
❌ used to helped

---

### 2. **help someone (to) do something**

= help someone do something

**帮助某人做某事**

例如：

- I helped my friend study English.  
    我帮助朋友学习英语。

这里：

> help her in the kitchen

= 帮她做厨房里的事情（比如做饭、洗菜等）

---

### 3. **whenever**

= every time that; any time when

**每当；无论什么时候**

例如：

- Whenever I feel tired, I take a short break.  
    每当我感觉累的时候，我都会休息一下。

---

### 4. **have the chance**

= have an opportunity

**有机会**

例如：

- I want to travel abroad whenever I have the chance.  
    我想有机会的时候去国外旅行。

---

## Common collocations:

- used to do something — 过去常常做某事
- have the chance to do something — 有机会做某事
- whenever possible — 只要可能
- help someone with something — 帮助某人做某事
- work in the kitchen — 在厨房工作

---

## My sentence:

I used to study economics whenever I had the chance because I wanted to understand how businesses work.

过去只要有机会，我就会学习经济学，因为我想理解企业是如何运作的。

---

## Sentence structure:

整个句子：

> I used to help her in the kitchen **whenever I had the chance**.

主句：

> I used to help her in the kitchen  
> 我过去常常在厨房帮助她

时间状语：

> whenever I had the chance  
> 每当我有机会的时候

可以理解为：

> **只要有机会，我过去就会帮她。**

这里的 **whenever** 比 **when** 更强调“每一次”。

比较：

- **When I had the chance**  
    当我有机会时（可能某一次）
    
- **Whenever I had the chance**  
    每当我有机会时（反复发生）✅
## 2.I also took a couple of cooking classes to learn how to do other kinds of cooking.

**Meaning:**  
I also attended two or a few cooking classes to learn different types of cooking skills.

**中文：**  
我还参加了几节烹饪课，学习如何做其他类型的菜。

---

## Key expressions:

### 1. **take a class / take classes**

= attend a class

**参加课程；上课**

例如：

- take an English class  
    上英语课
    
- take a finance class  
    上金融课
    

注意：

这里的 **take** 不是“拿”，而是“参加”。

---

### 2. **a couple of**

= two or a small number of

**两个；几个；少量**

这里：

> a couple of cooking classes

= 几节烹饪课 / 两三节烹饪课

---

### 3. **to learn how to do...**

= in order to learn the way to do something

**为了学习如何做……**

例如：

- I study Python to learn how to analyze data.  
    我学习 Python 是为了学习如何分析数据。

---

### 4. **other kinds of cooking**

= other types of cooking

**其他类型的烹饪方式**

例如：

- different kinds of food  
    不同种类的食物
    
- other kinds of skills  
    其他类型的技能
    

---

## Common collocations:

- take a class — 上课
- take a cooking class — 上烹饪课
- learn how to do something — 学习如何做某事
- different kinds of skills — 不同类型的技能
- improve cooking skills — 提升厨艺

---

## My sentence:

I took a couple of finance classes to learn how to analyze financial statements.

我参加了几门金融课程，学习如何分析财务报表。

---

## Sentence structure:

这个句子的核心：

> I took a couple of cooking classes **to learn how to do other kinds of cooking**.

结构：

**主句 + to do（目的）**

也就是：

> 我参加烹饪课，**目的是学习其他烹饪方法**。

类似：

- I study AI **to learn how to build intelligent systems**.  
    我学习 AI 是为了学习如何构建智能系统。
    
- I read financial reports **to learn how companies create value**.  
    我阅读财报是为了学习企业如何创造价值。
    

---

### 小提醒：

这里的 **how to do other kinds of cooking** 有一点口语化。

更自然的表达：

> learn how to cook different kinds of dishes

= 学习如何做不同种类的菜

因为 **cooking** 本身已经表示“烹饪”，所以 **do cooking** 不如 **cook dishes** 常见。
