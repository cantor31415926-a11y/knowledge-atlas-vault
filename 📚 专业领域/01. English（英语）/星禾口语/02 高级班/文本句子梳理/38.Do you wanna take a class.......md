
### would rather — 宁愿
## 1.would you rather learn an Asian language or a European one?

**Meaning:**  
Which do you prefer to learn: an Asian language or a European language?

**中文：**  
你更愿意学一门亚洲语言，还是一门欧洲语言？

**Course sentence:**  
Would you rather learn an Asian language or a European one for your future career?

**Common collocations:**

- would you rather A or B — 你更愿意 A 还是 B
- I would rather A than B — 我宁愿 A，而不愿 B
- would rather learn — 更愿意学习
- would rather choose — 更愿意选择

**My sentence:**  
I would rather learn a European language than another Asian language because it may help me access more international business resources.

我**更愿意学一门欧洲语言，而不是另一门亚洲语言**，因为它可能帮助我接触更多国际商业资源。

### 这里的 one 是什么？

**one = language**

所以：

> an Asian language or a European **one**

其实就是：

> an Asian language or a European **language**

为了避免重复 **language**，用 **one** 代替。


## 2.they have one on landscape photography and another on making videos

**Meaning:**  
They offer one course/class about landscape photography, and another course/class about making videos.

**中文：**  
他们有一门关于**风景摄影**的课程，另外还有一门关于**视频制作**的课程。

**Key expressions:**

- one ... and another ... — 一个……，另一个……
- landscape photography — 风景摄影
- make videos — 制作视频
- a course on/about something — 关于某主题的课程

**Course sentence:**  
They have one on landscape photography and another on making videos.

这里的 **one** 和 **another** 都是在代替前面已经提到过的 **course/class**，避免重复。

完整说法其实是：

> They have one **course** on landscape photography and another **course** on making videos.

**My sentence:**  
The school has one course on AI and another on financial analysis.

学校有一门关于 **AI** 的课程，另外还有一门关于**金融分析**的课程。
